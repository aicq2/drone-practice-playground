How to fly

  1. Lift off. On your phone, push the left stick up. On your Mac, hold R. Down is F, or pull the stick down.
  2. Turn. Left stick sideways, or Q and E on the keyboard.
  3. Move. Right stick glides you forward, back and sideways. On the keyboard use W A S D or the arrow keys.
  4. Score. Fly through a ring at its height to collect it. Watch the altitude readout at the top.
  5. Recharge. The battery drains as you fly. Land gently on the H pad to top it up.
A feel, not a flight model This is a practice playground made for fun. It is not an official DJI tool and it does not copy how any real drone handles. Real drones are heavier, faster and drift with wind, so treat this as a gentle first taste only.
